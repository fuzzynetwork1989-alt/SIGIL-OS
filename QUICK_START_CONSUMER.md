# SIGIL OS Quick Start Guide

**Get started with your AI companion in 3 minutes**

---

## Step 1: Install SIGIL OS

### Windows Users
1. Download `SIGIL-OS-Setup.exe` from our website
2. Double-click the downloaded file
3. Click "Install" in the setup wizard
4. Wait for installation to complete
5. Click "Finish" to launch SIGIL OS

### macOS Users
1. Download `SIGIL-OS.dmg` from our website
2. Open the downloaded file
3. Drag SIGIL OS to your Applications folder
4. Open Applications and double-click SIGIL OS

### Linux Users
1. Download `SIGIL-OS.AppImage` from our website
2. Open your terminal
3. Run: `chmod +x SIGIL-OS.AppImage`
4. Run: `./SIGIL-OS.AppImage`

---

## Step 2: First Launch

When you first open SIGIL OS, you'll see a welcome screen explaining the key features:

- **Long-term Memory** - I remember our conversations
- **Mission Tracking** - Track your projects and goals
- **Dream Weaving™** - Self-evolving intelligence
- **Privacy First** - Your data stays local

Click **"Get Started"** to begin.

---

## Step 3: Start Chatting

That's it! You're ready to use SIGIL OS.

1. Type your message in the box at the bottom
2. Press Enter or click "Send"
3. Your AI companion will respond

**Try asking:**
- "Hello! Can you help me?"
- "What can you do?"
- "Help me plan my day"

## 5 Things to Try First

### 1. Have a Conversation
Go to Chat and ask: "Tell me about yourself"

### 2. Customize Your Avatar
Go to Avatar and change the eye color, hair style, or clothing

### 3. Create a Mission
Go to Missions and click "+ Add New Mission"
- Name: "Learn Something New"
- Description: "Learn a new skill this month"

### 4. Check Your Memory
Go to Memory to see what SIGIL remembers about you

### 5. Change the Theme
Go to Settings → Appearance → Theme
Try the Light, Blue, Green, or Purple theme
