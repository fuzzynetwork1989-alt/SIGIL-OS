### Full Description (4000 characters max)

**SIGIL OS - Your AI Companion with Self-Evolving Intelligence**

SIGIL OS is a revolutionary AI companion that doesn't just respond—it learns, reflects, and evolves. Powered by Dream Weaving™ technology, SIGIL OS enters dream-like states to generate novel insights, reflect on its own reasoning, and adapt its cognitive pathways to better serve you.

**Key Features:**

🤖 **TrueHD Avatar Voice Assistant**
- Lifelike digital avatar that responds to your voice
- Real-time emotion expression and lip-syncing
- Customizable appearance (realistic, stylized, or procedural)

🧠 **Dream Weaving™ Self-Evolution**
- Revolutionary cognitive system that learns from conversations
- Self-reflection to detect and correct biases
- Controlled evolution with full rollback capability
- Every change is warranted, reversible, and auditable

🎨 **Beautiful Customizable Interface**
- 5 stunning themes (Light, Dark, Blue, Green, Purple)
- Custom avatar morphing based on conversation context
- Smooth animations and intuitive controls

🥽 **Meta Quest 3 Mixed Reality**
- Experience SIGIL OS in full mixed reality
- Spatial anchors to pin your companion anywhere
- Hand gesture controls for natural interaction
- Passthrough mode to see your real environment

🔒 **Privacy-First Design**
- All data stored locally by default
- No telemetry without your consent
- Open-source code for full transparency
- Cryptographic signing for all operations

**Use Cases:**

- **Personal Assistant:** Daily tasks, scheduling, reminders
- **Learning Companion:** Explain complex topics, tutor you
- **Creative Partner:** Brainstorm ideas, write stories
- **Emotional Support:** Listen, empathize, provide perspective
- **Research Assistant:** Summarize information, find insights

**Technical Highlights:**

- Runs locally on your device (no cloud required after setup)
- Powered by Qwen 7B AI model (13+ billion parameters)
- Rust-based for maximum performance and security
- Slint UI framework for smooth, native feel
- Supports Windows, macOS, Linux, and Meta Quest 3

**What Makes SIGIL OS Different:**

Unlike other AI assistants, SIGIL OS features Dream Weaving™—a groundbreaking self-evolving cognitive system. While other AIs remain static, SIGIL OS learns and adapts over time, with full control and transparency. Every evolutionary step is tracked, requires your approval, and can be rolled back.

**Privacy & Security:**

SIGIL OS is built on cryptographic principles. Every cognitive operation is signed, tracked, and auditable. Your data stays on your device by default. You control what, if anything, is shared.

**Open Source:**

SIGIL OS is fully open-source. Inspect the code, contribute, or run your own modified version. Transparency is a core principle.

## Store-Specific Requirements

### Apple App Store

**Additional Requirements:**
- App Store Connect account ($99/year)
- Developer Program enrollment
- TestFlight beta testing (recommended)
- App Review Guidelines compliance
- In-app purchase (if offering premium features)

**Review Notes for App Review:**
```
SIGIL OS is an AI companion app that runs locally on the user's device.
No cloud services are required for basic functionality.
All AI processing happens on-device using the Qwen 7B model.
The app includes Dream Weaving, a self-evolving cognitive system that
learns from user interactions with full user control and rollback capability.

Test Account: N/A (no login required)
Demo Instructions:
1. Launch app
2. Type "Hello" in chat
3. Observe avatar response
4. Go to Settings → Dream Weaving to see self-evolution features
5. Customize avatar in Avatar settings
```

### Google Play Store

**Additional Requirements:**
- Google Play Developer account ($25 one-time)
- Content rating questionnaire
- Privacy policy URL
- Target audience declaration
- Data safety section

**Data Safety Section:**
- **Data Collection:** Local storage only, no collection without consent
- **Data Sharing:** No sharing with third parties
- **Data Security:** Local encryption, no cloud transmission
- **User Control:** Full control over data, can delete anytime

### Meta Quest Store

**Additional Requirements:**
- Meta for Developers account
- Quest 3 device for testing
- Concept approval
- ITP (Incoming Transmission Policy) compliance
- Performance review

**Quest-Specific Notes:**
- Requires Quest 3 (Passthrough API)
- Hand tracking enabled
- Spatial mapping used
- Voice input via Quest microphone
- Mixed Reality passthrough mode
